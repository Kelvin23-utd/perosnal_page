I am a PhD student in Computer Science at The University of Texas at Dallas. My research focuses on mobile sensing, low-power edge systems, and multimodal perception. I build and evaluate sensing systems that connect embedded hardware, machine learning, and real-world deployment.

My work spans the full stack of a sensing system: hardware integration, firmware, data collection, on-device machine learning, power measurement, and deployment. The platforms I work with include cameras, microphones, GNSS receivers, microcontrollers, and single-board computers.

Research

Low-Power Multimodal Field Sensing

Ongoing
Question
How long can an autonomous sensor node keep observing on a fixed energy budget, and where does that budget actually go?
Approach
A two-tier node in which sub-watt microcontroller sensors wake an inference-class board only when needed, combining camera, acoustic, motion, and GNSS sensing. Every sensing and compute stage is measured with instrumented power metering rather than estimated.
Status
Validated end to end on battery in unattended bench runs; moving toward field deployment.

On-Device LLM Reasoning for Sensing

Active
Question
Can a small language model running entirely on a phone do useful semantic reasoning over sensor data, inside the memory, latency, and energy budget of that device?
Approach
Bound the model's role inside a deterministic pipeline so that its call count and input size stay fixed, then measure latency, memory, and energy on real hardware instead of on a workstation proxy.
Status
Hestia is under review at ACM SenSys. The efficiency measurement study that motivated it appeared at FMSys ’25.

Cross-Modal Transfer for GNSS Gesture Sensing

Ongoing
Question
Can representations learned from data-rich WiFi sensing transfer to GNSS-based sensing, which is fundamentally data-scarce?
Approach
Build bridges between the two modalities through shared physical representations, and characterise when such transfer succeeds and when it fails rather than assuming it works.
Status
Ongoing. Experiments are in progress and there are no results to report yet.
