01. About Me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science (Systems Track) at the University of Texas at Dallas, under the supervision of Dr. Yi Ding. I completed my bachelor's degree in Computer Science from the University of Birmingham (First Class Honours, 2022) and my master's degree in Applied Computational Science & Engineering from Imperial College London (Merit, 2023).

My research interests focus on mobile computing, on-device intelligence, and edge–cloud systems, with particular emphasis on deploying and optimizing large language models for resource-constrained environments. I'm also interested in acoustic sensing, privacy-preserving systems, and human activity recognition.

02. Research

Current 01/2025 – Present

Hestia: Privacy-Preserving Home Routine Recognition

Designed a hybrid edge–central system that reconstructs daily routines from sparse acoustic detections using commodity microphones and on-device LLM reasoning, with no raw audio leaving the home.

  • Hierarchical temporal clustering with bounded on-device LLM inference
  • Continual self-adaptation via LLM pseudo-labels
  • Deployed across two real homes, matching or exceeding camera/WiFi/IMU baselines
Acoustic Sensing On-Device LLM Privacy Edge Computing
Completed 01/2025 – 03/2025

Efficiency of LLM Applications on Mobile Devices

Evaluated efficiency tradeoffs across mobile, edge, and cloud deployments for LLM-based applications, benchmarking latency, energy, and accuracy under multiple model compression strategies.

  • Built AutoLife-Lite: lightweight LLM app for real-time context inference from sensor data
  • Quantified performance gaps between deployment strategies
  • Established design guidelines for resource-constrained LLM systems
Mobile LLM Benchmarking Model Compression Energy Efficiency
